Book Donation Programme (4-step timeline)

1

Collect

We gather donated books from law firms, schools, and individuals.

2

Sort & Curate

Volunteers check condition and age-appropriateness.

3

Box & Deliver

Books are packed and transported to rural schools.

4

Set up reading corner

We help schools create a welcoming reading space.
